# Xooreva

Xooreva is a content studio from Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic, operated by WWS SRL. We create original short videos about the history of Dominican music and Dominican culture for Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.

xoovera-app is our internal tool to publish our own videos to our TikTok account.
