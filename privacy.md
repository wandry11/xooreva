# Xooreva - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Xooreva ("we", "us"), a content studio operated by WWS SRL in Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic, handles information when you use the xoovera-app integration with TikTok and other platforms.

## Information we collect

When the account owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we receive basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) and an access token issued by TikTok. We do not collect information from other TikTok users, followers or viewers.

## How we use information

We use this information only to identify the connected account and to upload and publish videos created by Xooreva to that same account through the TikTok Content Posting API. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely on our own systems and are used only for publishing our content. We keep the information only while the account remains connected.

## Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time from your TikTok settings (Manage app permissions). After disconnection, or upon request, we delete the stored tokens and profile data.

## Children

This service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Xooreva through our Facebook page or the contact listed at https://wandry11.github.io/xooreva/
